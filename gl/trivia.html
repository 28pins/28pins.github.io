Statement Answer Optional Context to Read
Job was an Israelite. False The Book of Job gives no historical context.
Judah was Jacob's firstborn. False Reuben was.
Abraham had two sons. False After Ishmael and Isaac, he had more children with other wives.
The Mosaic Law points to Jesus and His Atonement. True It was designed to point the Israelites to Him and symbolize His mission.
Samson and David fought and killed lions. True Both credited God with helping them.
The Mosaic Law was wicked. False Jesus condemned those—like the Pharisees—who perverted it, but He commended those who followed it.
Rahab was Jesus"s ancestor. True Through Boaz.
Moses ate a meal with God. True He and some Israelite leaders did on Mount Sinai.
Dan and Bethlehem were the famous boundaries of Israel. False The traditional boundaries were Dan and Beersheba.
David beat Goliath. True We can all beat giants with God"s help.
Babylon was an ancient empire with no modern significance. False In scriptures, it represents the world or the devil"s kingdom.
Serious sins, like those of Saul, David, and Solomon, are the only thing that can separate us from the love of God. False Nothing can, not even our own choices.
